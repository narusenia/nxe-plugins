# NXE Basic

**Six plugins for vocals and the mix.** Basic is the series that shapes a
sound rather than reinventing it: doubling, presence, sparkle, air, distance
and resonance control, each as a focused tool you can reach for on any track.

| Plugin | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Doubler 2** | Multi-voice doubler — 2, 4 or 8 detuned, delayed, humanized voices from one source |
| **Velour 2** | Vocal presence saturator — three parallel harmonic generators added to an untouched dry path |
| **Sparkleur 2** | Multiband dynamics with a transient-gated harmonic generator — five bands, upward and downward |
| **Air 2** | Signal-driven texture generator — harmonics and noise placed where the source is not, following what it does |
| **Diorama 2** | A vocal's distance as one knob — early reflections and a direct path that is itself processed, no reverb tail |
| **Pumice** | Dynamic resonance suppression for a single vocal, with six nodes you place by hand over what it is doing |

## Made to sit side by side

Every window shares one shape: the controls in a sidebar, one large figure
that shows what the plugin is doing as you change it, and one accent colour
per plugin from a single family. Open several at once and they read as one
set.

Every plugin ships with factory presets and saves your own.

## Formats

- **macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): CLAP, VST3 and AU
- **Windows**: CLAP and VST3
